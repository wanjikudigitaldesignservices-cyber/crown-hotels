# Crown Hotels Kenya — Kitchen Display & Waiter Quickstart Guide

This quickstart guide is designed to be printed or laminated for kitchen line cooks, expeditors, and floor servers.

---

## 👨‍🍳 1. Kitchen Display System (KDS) — `/kitchen`

The Kitchen Display System runs in any web browser on an Android tablet or kitchen touchscreen monitor mounted at the expeditor station.

### Understanding the Ticket Board
- **New Incoming Orders**:
  - Whenever a guest submits an order via M-Pesa, Pay at Table, or Room Charge, a pleasant two-tone chime alerts the kitchen.
  - New orders appear in the **Received** column with a pulsating blue badge.
- **Elapsed Timer & Color Coding**:
  - `0 – 10 minutes`: **Green** (Normal pacing).
  - `10 – 20 minutes`: **Amber** (Approaching target window).
  - `> 20 minutes`: **Red** (Priority rush / overdue alert).
- **Special Notes & Allergens**:
  - Dietary restrictions (e.g. *NO PEANUTS*, *GLUTEN FREE*, *EXTRA CHILI*) appear highlighted in red high-contrast warning boxes.
- **Advancing Tickets**:
  - Tap **Start Preparing** when the chef begins cooking.
  - Tap **Mark as Ready** when the dishes are plated and under the heat lamp.
  - *Instant synchronization*: Marking ready triggers a notification on the server's Waiter View and updates the guest's live phone tracker.

---

## 🍽️ 2. Waiter Service View — `/waiter`

Floor servers carry mobile devices or check the station terminal at `/waiter`.

### Two Primary Views

1. **Ready to Serve Column**:
   - Lists dishes plated and waiting in the pass.
   - Shows Table Number, Item Names, Modifiers (e.g., *Well Done + Ugali*), and Server Name.
   - Tap **Dispatched / Served** once the tray is placed in front of the guest.

2. **Table Assistance Alerts**:
   - **Call Waiter Alert**: Yellow banner showing table number and time elapsed.
   - **Request Bill Alert**: Purple banner showing table number and payment method preference (M-Pesa STK Push / Cash / Card).
   - Tap **Acknowledge Alert** once attending to the table to clear the notification.

---

## 🛎️ 3. Room Service Approvals (Front Desk View)

When an in-room dining guest selects **Charge to Room**:
1. Order appears with a **Pending Room Verification** flag.
2. Front desk cross-references the guest's folio in the PMS.
3. Tap **Approve Charge** to release the ticket to the kitchen, or **Decline** if the room deposit limit has been reached.
